Research Projects

From surgical robotics to fluid power and cardiovascular engineering.

RESEARCH DIRECTION 01

Continuum Laparoscopic Robot: Design, Modeling, and Control

We develop continuum laparoscopic robots that make surgical viewing more flexible and intuitive. Mechanical design decouples orientation and zoom, while gaze attention and surgical-tool tracking guide automatic field-of-view adjustment. Teleoperation and feedback control connect surgeon intent with robot motion.

Related publications

  • Automatic field-of-view Adjustment for Multi-surgical Tools of A Novel Continuum Laparoscopic Robot Measurement (2026)
  • GazeScope: A Framework of Gaze Attention-based Automatic Field-of-View Adjustment for Laparoscopic Robots IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (2025)
  • Automatic Field of View Adjustment of an RCM Constraint-Free Continuum Laparoscopic Robot 2024 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) (2024)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 02

MRI-Compatible Pneumatic Motor: Design, Modeling, and Control

We build MRI-compatible pneumatic actuators for robots operating inside magnetic imaging environments. Modular motion outputs and encoders support precise movement, while modeling and control address the dynamics introduced by long air tubes. Our goal is reliable position, velocity, and torque control for image-guided intervention.

Related publications

  • An MRI-Compatible Pneumatic Servo Motor: Position-Speed-Torque Control with Long Air Tubes IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics (2027)
  • Estimation of Unmeasurable States in an MRI-Safe Pneumatic Motor Enabling Torque-Based Servo Control Control Engineering Practice (2026)
  • Cornerstone for MRI-compatible Robots: A Continuous Pneumatic Actuator with Easy-to-Connect Prismatic/Revolute and Bidirectional Encoder Modules Measurement (2024)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 03

Eye Gaze Estimation: Algorithm Design and Applications

We study how eye movements can become a natural interface for robotic systems. Our work combines eye detection, gaze estimation, and visual attention with applications in needle deployment, laparoscopic viewing, and helmet aiming. The aim is to translate where a person looks into useful robotic assistance.

Related publications

  • An Eye-Gaze-Controlled Needle Deployment Robot: Design, Modeling, and Experimental Evaluation IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement (2024)
  • Imitating the Human Visual System for Scanpath Predicting 2024 IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP) (2024)
  • A Joint Cascaded Framework for Simultaneous Eye State Eye Center and Gaze Estimation 2022 26th International Conference on Pattern Recognition (ICPR) (2022)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 04

Parallel Robot: Design, Modeling, and Control

We design compact parallel mechanisms for image-guided intervention and robotic endoscopy. Analytical kinematics and learning-based models address the challenges of coupled motion and multiple forward-kinematic solutions. Visual feedback supports precise positioning and automated surgical viewing.

Related publications

  • A CDE Operator Learning Framework for Forward Kinematics of Heterogeneous Parallel Robots Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation (2026)
  • Modeling, Analysis and Evaluation of a Novel Compact 6 DoF 3-RRRS Needle Biopsy Robot Mathematics (2024)
  • A Partition-Learning-Selection-Augmentation (PLSA) Framework to Solve Forward Kinematics of Parallel Robots 2025 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) (2025)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 05

Trajectory Planning: Optimizing Energy Consumption and Completion Time in UAV-Assisted IoT Networks

We optimize how UAVs move and collect data in Internet-of-Things networks. Trajectory planning balances aircraft energy, sensing-device energy, and mission completion time while respecting communication requirements. Simulations compare flight paths and the tradeoffs between speed and efficiency.

Related publications

  • Efficient Trajectory Planning for Optimizing Energy Consumption and Completion Time in UAV-Assisted IoT Networks Mathematics (2023)
  • Energy-Efficient Trajectory Planning for Smart Sensing in IoT Networks Using Quadrotor UAVs Sensors (2022)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 06

Pneumatic Actuator, Miniature Hydraulic Power Unit, and Soft Robotics

We explore fluid-powered actuation for compliant robots that interact with people and their environment. Pneumatic mechanisms provide motion, and miniature hydraulic sources deliver compact power for soft robotic systems. This work connects actuator design, prototype fabrication, and experimental evaluation.

Related publications

  • A Modularly Designed Miniature Fluidic Power Source for Soft Robots 2024 International Conference on Frontiers of Robotics and Software Engineering (FRSE) (2024)
  • V-tube the First Prototype of Pneumatic Actuated Soft Nasal Swab for COVID-19 Screening 2022 China Automation Congress (CAC) (2022)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 07

Hemocompatibility Study of A Centrifugal Cardiac Assist System and An Axial Microcatheter Support System

We investigate how mechanical circulatory support devices affect blood. In-vitro circulation loops enable comparisons of device hemocompatibility, including hemolysis and shear-related effects. This work evaluates cardiac-assist technologies and informs the design of safer blood-contacting systems.

Related publications

  • Impella 5.5 Versus Centrimag A Head to Head Comparison of Device Hemocompatibility ASAIO Journal (2020)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 08

Hemocompatibility Study of In-vitro Circulatory Loop Components

We examine how the test loop itself can influence measurements of blood damage. Connectors, bends, and loop geometry can introduce shear-related artifacts that complicate device comparisons. Experiments and computational models help distinguish device effects from those introduced by the testing system.

Related publications

  • Circulatory Loop Design and Constituent Components Introduce Artifacts Impacting In-Vitro Evaluation of Ventricular Assist Device Thrombogenicity Artificial Organs (2019)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 09

Design of Novel MCS Devices and Hemocompatibility Tests

We design mechanical circulatory support devices with alternative blood-propulsion mechanisms. Valveless pulsatile and peristaltic concepts seek to restore useful flow while addressing the limitations of conventional pumps. Modeling, prototyping, and hemocompatibility evaluation guide development.

Related publications

  • Design and Evaluation of A Pulsatile Flow Rotary Vane Blood Pump Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers, Part C: Journal of Mechanical Engineering Science (2023)
  • Design, Modeling, and Experimental Characterization of A Valveless Pulsatile Flow Mechanical Circulatory Support Device Journal of Medical Devices - Trans of the ASME (2021)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 10

Modeling of Interaction between Circulatory Support Device and Cardiovascular System

We model the interaction between circulatory support devices and the cardiovascular system. Bond-graph models connect device behavior with pressure, flow, and cardiac dynamics. These models support device sizing, control design, and evaluation alongside physical mock-circulation systems.

Related publications

  • Bond Graph Modeling of Mechanical Circulatory Support (MCS) Cardiovascular System Interactions Journal of Biomechanical Engineering - Trans of the ASME (2020)
RESEARCH DIRECTION 11

A Novel Alternating Flow Variable Displacement Hydraulic Pump

We develop alternating-flow hydraulic pumps that vary displacement while targeting efficient operation across a wide operating range. First-principles models connect the mechanical design to dynamic behavior. Prototype experiments provide a basis for validating models and evaluating performance.

Related publications

  • Design, Dynamic Modelling and Experiment Validation of a Novel Alternating Flow Variable Displacement Hydraulic Pump IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics (2019)
  • Dynamic Model of a Novel Alternating Flow (AF) Hydraulic Pump ASME/BATH Symposium on Fluid Power and Motion Control (FPMC) (2017)
